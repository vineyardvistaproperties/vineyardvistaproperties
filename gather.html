Gathering, without the venue feeling.

Vineyard Vista is a home first. Our private gatherings are intentionally small, relaxed, and personal—suited to a long lunch, an afternoon shower, a celebratory toast, or a few meaningful hours together.

Select day-rate access offers the chance to enjoy the property without an overnight stay when available, creating an easy setting for hosts who want something more intimate than a traditional event venue.
