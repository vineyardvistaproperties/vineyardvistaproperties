Vineyard Vista Policies

The operating policies, rental standards, house standards and property-management requirements that govern Vineyard Vista.

01

Property Operations

The core operating standards for managing the home day to day, including delegated authority, escalation, readiness, access and record keeping.

Property Manager Mandate

Day-to-day authority

Handle routine property operations, vendor coordination, guest support, approved purchasing, inspections and ordinary repairs needed to keep the home safe, functional and arrival-ready.

Issue escalation

Escalate safety concerns, material property damage, recurring system failures, anticipated work above delegated authority and any issue likely to affect a confirmed stay.

Property readiness

Maintain the home in a consistently arrival-ready condition, with particular attention to cleanliness, supplies, systems, access, exterior presentation and any open maintenance items.

Record keeping

Maintain vendor records, invoices, inspection notes, maintenance history, warranties, access permissions and other operating records needed to manage the property effectively.

Spending & Approval Authority

AmountOperating Standard
Up to $1,500The property manager may authorize ordinary necessary repairs and approved operating purchases without advance approval.
$1,501–$10,000Obtain approval before committing to the work or purchase.
Above $10,000Written approval is required before proceeding.
EmergencyTake only the action reasonably necessary to protect life, safety or prevent additional material damage, then notify Vineyard Vista Properties promptly.

Operating Priorities

Protect the property

Address active leaks, loss of heat or cooling, electrical or plumbing concerns, storm exposure, security issues and other conditions that could worsen if left unattended.

Protect the guest experience

Resolve guest-impacting issues promptly and communicate clearly when service, repair access or an alternate solution is required during a stay.

Preserve house standards

Use approved products, replacement standards and vendors whenever available. Material substitutions or changes to the established house standard require approval.

Document material issues

Record damage, significant repairs, vendor findings, replacement decisions and recurring concerns so the property history remains complete and easy to reference.

02

Rentals & Agreements

All paid occupancy is centralized through Vineyard Vista. This section governs stay intake, agreement execution, pet approvals, deposits, guest communications, turnover and post-stay damage handling. Event-specific operating standards are maintained separately under Events.

Core Rental Rule

Every paid stay runs through the LLC framework.

No cash rentals, owner-created listings, side agreements, informal paid stays or direct payment to an individual owner. Every stay uses the approved booking record, rental agreement, payment process, deposits, insurance requirements and central calendar.

Standard Booking Workflow

Inquiry & fit check

Confirm dates, party size, maximum occupancy, intended use of the home, pet request and whether any gathering beyond normal occupancy is contemplated.

Pricing & terms

Confirm rental rate, required non-refundable deposit, security deposit, $500 cleaning fee, any additional fees and payment schedule before issuing documents.

Agreement package

Issue the Rental Agreement plus the Pet Addendum and/or Vineyard Vista Properties Gathering Addendum to Rental Agreement when applicable. No pet or gathering approval is complete until the applicable addendum is fully approved and executed.

Collect funds

Collect deposit and required fees. The current form provides for late fees of $100 per day and allows cancellation where balances remain unpaid more than seven days after the due date.

Pre-arrival verification

Confirm signed documents, full payment, security deposits, approved occupants, pet/gathering details, guest contact information and any special operating notes.

Guest access

Issue time-limited or reservation-specific access instructions and the About This House guide only after the reservation is cleared for arrival.

Turnover & inspection

Housekeeping resets the property and reports missing items, damage, stains, unusual cleaning needs and maintenance issues before the next occupancy.

Deposit disposition

Document damages and extraordinary cleaning. Apply recoverable renter costs against the relevant deposit first, then pursue any remaining contractual recovery if necessary.

Rental Agreement — Key Operating Terms

Occupancy

Stay is limited to the approved maximum occupancy, including children. Exceeding the limit may result in termination and eviction without refund.

Purpose of stay

Residential/vacation use only. Any gathering beyond normal occupancy requires prior written approval and an executed Vineyard Vista Properties Gathering Addendum to Rental Agreement.

Pets

Pets require prior approval and an executed Pet Addendum. The base rental agreement states a $250 non-refundable pet fee plus an additional deposit.

Smoking

Smoking, vaping and electronic cigarettes are prohibited inside and within 25 feet of the property. Residual smoke odor may result in loss or application of the security deposit.

Damage

Renter is responsible for damage caused by the renter, guests or pets beyond normal wear and tear and must report issues promptly.

Cancellation

Current template: 60+ days before arrival forfeits the deposit but not the remaining balance; within 30 days the full rental amount is due unless the property is re-rented for the same period.

Pet Approval Workflow

Collect pet profile

Record pet type, breed, name, weight and age. The current Pet Addendum allows one pet; any additional pet requires separate prior written consent.

Review suitability

Confirm the proposed pet can comply with the house rules: no disruptive behavior or aggression, supervised on property, crated or secured if left inside, and leashed or under direct control outdoors.

Approve in writing

Do not rely on a text or verbal exception. Complete and sign the Pet Addendum identifying the specifically approved animal.

Collect pet charges

Collect the $250 non-refundable pet fee and one $1,000 refundable pet deposit per reservation, regardless of the number of approved pets. The addendum provides for return of the deposit within seven days after checkout if no damage is incurred.

Record operating notes

Flag the booking for housekeeping. Pets are not permitted on furniture or beds; renter must dispose of pet waste and is responsible for pet-related damage or extra cleaning.

Pet approval checklist

  • One pet maximum unless separately approved in writing
  • Type / breed / name / weight / age documented
  • No known disruptive or aggressive behavior
  • Supervision and crate/security requirements acknowledged
  • Leash/direct-control rule acknowledged
  • No furniture or beds
  • $250 fee collected
  • One $1,000 pet deposit collected for the reservation
  • Pet Addendum executed
  • Housekeeping notified

Housekeeping & Turnovers

Peak season

Professional weekly turnover cleaning throughout June, July and August is mandatory and shared, regardless of whether the departing occupancy was an owner or renter.

Turnover inspection

Housekeeping reports breakage, missing inventory, stains, abnormal depletion, pet/gathering-related cleaning, maintenance concerns and anything that could affect the next arrival.

Reset standard

Fresh linens and towels, cleaned kitchen/baths, replenished approved supplies, reset furniture and equipment, secure owner closets, thermostat reset, lock check and visible exterior review.

Off-season owner stays

Stay-specific cleaning and guest costs are charged to the owner using or facilitating the stay.

Unified Event Workflow

All event requests—whether day-use or associated with an overnight rental—follow the same seven steps below. An approved rental does not automatically approve an event; any gathering beyond normal occupancy requires separate written event approval. The agreement package varies by booking type.

Review the request

Confirm the event type, date, start and end time, expected guest count, requested property areas, vehicles and parking plan, vendors, alcohol service, decorations, rentals, setup needs and any overnight component. If material details are missing, obtain them before approval.

Approve the event & set terms

Validate availability against owner use, confirmed stays, maintenance blocks and turnover needs. If suitable, document the approved event type, hours, maximum guest count, approved areas, parking, vendors, alcohol and decor conditions, applicable event charges, refundable event security deposit and any special restrictions.

Prepare & execute the agreements

Day event:
Private Event / Day-Use Agreement

Rental + gathering:
Rental Agreement
Vineyard Vista Properties Gathering Addendum to Rental Agreement

If a pet is also approved:
Pet Addendum

For an overnight stay, the Gathering Addendum applies only to the approved gathering and does not replace or expand the Rental Agreement. Do not treat the gathering as approved until every required agreement has been fully executed.

Collect required funds

Collect the applicable nonrefundable event charges and the refundable event security deposit after the required agreements are signed. Keep event charges itemized separately from ordinary rental charges, and do not count refundable deposits as projected gross revenue.

Complete the pre-event review

Before the event, reconfirm the final guest count, event timing, vendor roster and arrival windows, parking plan, approved property areas, alcohol arrangements, decorations, rentals, setup plan and access instructions. Any material change requires property-manager approval.

  • No unauthorized gatherings or attendance above the approved guest maximum.
  • Use only the property areas specifically approved for the event.
  • Caterers and other vendors require prior approval.
  • No blocked driveways or neighbor disruption.
  • No nails, tacks or wall adhesives; all decorations and rentals must be removed after the event.
  • No smoking, vaping or electronic cigarettes inside or within 25 feet of the property.
  • The requester is responsible for guest conduct, event-related damage and extraordinary cleaning.

Host the event

Coordinate vendor access and property readiness, secure restricted areas and provide only the temporary access needed. During the event, enforce the approved hours, guest count, property areas, parking and vendor plan. Keep noise controlled and maintain the property quiet period of 10:00 PM–8:00 AM unless a different written condition has been expressly approved.

Inspect & close out

After the event, confirm vendors have removed materials, rentals, decorations and waste; inspect all approved event areas; photograph and document any damage or extraordinary cleaning; open maintenance items where needed; and record any security-deposit deduction by category, amount, description and supporting documentation. If no deductions apply, process the refundable event security deposit for return, reset the house and close the event record.

Property Management

The working operating page for the physical house: property manager responsibilities, housekeeping, access, vendors, preventive maintenance, seasonal operations, annual repairs and capital planning.

Access & Security

Credential structure

Use separate credentials for owners, property manager, housekeeping, approved vendors and rental guests wherever the lock platform permits.

Guest access

Rental codes should be reservation-specific, time-limited or changed immediately after checkout.

Vendor access

Prefer vendor-specific or temporary codes. Permanent master codes should be tightly restricted.

Owner storage

Locked owner closets/storage should remain secured during all rental occupancy and vendor visits unless access is specifically required.

Approved Vendor Roster

Maintain enough local vendor depth that one unavailable contractor does not leave the house unsupported, particularly during peak season or after storms.

Repairs & Capital Planning

Repair log

Every non-trivial repair should capture date reported, issue, vendor, cost, resolution, warranty and whether recurrence is expected.

Deferred work

Cosmetic or non-urgent items that can wait should be grouped for off-season execution rather than disrupting guest or owner occupancy.

Capital register

Major system replacement, structural work, renovation and other capital projects should show budget, vote/approval, vendor, schedule and completion status.

Maintenance blocks

The property manager may block reasonable dates for maintenance, deep cleaning or inspections; planned work should avoid scheduled private-use periods and confirmed rentals whenever practical.
