Rentals & Agreements

All paid occupancy is centralized through Vineyard Vista. This page governs booking intake, agreement execution, pet and event approvals, deposits, guest communications, turnover and post-stay damage handling.

Core Rental Rule

Every paid stay runs through the LLC framework.

No cash rentals, owner-created listings, side agreements, informal paid stays or direct payment to an individual owner. Every stay uses the approved booking record, rental agreement, payment process, deposits, insurance requirements and central calendar.

Standard Booking Workflow

Inquiry & fit check

Confirm dates, party size, maximum occupancy, intended use of the home, pet request and whether any party/event/gathering is contemplated.

Pricing & terms

Confirm rental rate, required non-refundable deposit, security deposit, $500 cleaning fee, any additional fees and payment schedule before issuing documents.

Agreement package

Issue the Rental Agreement plus Pet Addendum and/or Party Addendum when applicable. No pet or event approval is complete until the applicable addendum is fully approved and executed.

Collect funds

Collect deposit and required fees. The current form provides for late fees of $100 per day and allows cancellation where balances remain unpaid more than seven days after the due date.

Pre-arrival verification

Confirm signed documents, full payment, security deposits, approved occupants, pet/event details, guest contact information and any special operating notes.

Guest access

Issue time-limited or reservation-specific access instructions and the About This House guide only after the reservation is cleared for arrival.

Turnover & inspection

Housekeeping resets the property and reports missing items, damage, stains, unusual cleaning needs and maintenance issues before the next occupancy.

Deposit disposition

Document damages and extraordinary cleaning. Apply recoverable renter costs against the relevant deposit first, then pursue any remaining contractual recovery if necessary.

Rental Agreement — Key Operating Terms

Occupancy

Stay is limited to the approved maximum occupancy, including children. Exceeding the limit may result in termination and eviction without refund.

Purpose of stay

Residential/vacation use only. Events, parties and gatherings require prior written approval and the Party Addendum.

Pets

Pets require prior approval and an executed Pet Addendum. The base rental agreement states a $250 non-refundable pet fee plus an additional deposit.

Smoking

Smoking, vaping and electronic cigarettes are prohibited inside and within 25 feet of the property. Residual smoke odor may result in loss or application of the security deposit.

Damage

Renter is responsible for damage caused by the renter, guests or pets beyond normal wear and tear and must report issues promptly.

Cancellation

Current template: 60+ days before arrival forfeits the deposit but not the remaining balance; within 30 days the full rental amount is due unless the property is re-rented for the same period.

Pet Approval Workflow

Collect pet profile

Record pet type, breed, name, weight and age. The current Pet Addendum allows one pet; any additional pet requires separate prior written consent.

Review suitability

Confirm the proposed pet can comply with the house rules: no disruptive behavior or aggression, supervised on property, crated or secured if left inside, and leashed or under direct control outdoors.

Approve in writing

Do not rely on a text or verbal exception. Complete and sign the Pet Addendum identifying the specifically approved animal.

Collect pet charges

Collect the $250 non-refundable pet fee and $1,000 refundable pet deposit. The addendum provides for return of the deposit within seven days after checkout if no damage is incurred.

Record operating notes

Flag the booking for housekeeping. Pets are not permitted on furniture or beds; renter must dispose of pet waste and is responsible for pet-related damage or extra cleaning.

Pet approval checklist

  • One pet maximum unless separately approved in writing
  • Type / breed / name / weight / age documented
  • No known disruptive or aggressive behavior
  • Supervision and crate/security requirements acknowledged
  • Leash/direct-control rule acknowledged
  • No furniture or beds
  • $250 fee collected
  • $1,000 pet deposit collected
  • Pet Addendum executed
  • Housekeeping notified

Party / Event Approval Workflow

An event is an exception, not a default rental privilege. No party, event or gathering may occur without prior written consent and a completed Party Addendum.

Request full event details

Obtain type of event, date, start/end time, expected guest count, proposed property areas, parking needs, catering/vendors, alcohol service and decoration plans.

Operational review

Assess occupancy and guest limit, parking capacity, neighbor impact, permitted areas, vendor access, cleaning implications and whether the event is compatible with insurance and property rules.

Set event-specific conditions

Complete maximum guest count, approved property areas, parking limit, non-refundable party fee and any additional refundable security deposit. The template requires quiet consideration especially from 10:00 PM–8:00 AM.

Approve vendors in advance

Caterers and other vendors require prior owner approval. The renter remains responsible for ensuring all vendors comply with the Party Addendum and Rental Agreement.

Execute before confirming

Written owner approval, signed Party Addendum and payment of the event fee/deposit must occur before the event is treated as approved.

Post-event inspection

Inspect furniture, fixtures, landscaping and approved event areas. Extra carpet cleaning, trash removal, odor remediation or other event-related cleaning may be deducted from the security deposit or billed to the renter.

Non-negotiable event rules

  • No unauthorized parties or gatherings
  • Stay within approved guest limit
  • Use only approved areas
  • No blocked driveways or neighbor disruption
  • No damaging decorations—no nails, tacks or adhesives on walls
  • Alcohol must comply with law; disorderly conduct not tolerated
  • No smoking/vaping inside or within 25 feet of the property

Breach consequences

The current addendum permits immediate cancellation of the event, eviction, forfeiture of the security deposit and other applicable penalties for violations.

Guest Communication & Close-Out

Before arrival

  • Reservation confirmation
  • Payment and document status
  • Approved guest count
  • Pet/event confirmation if relevant
  • Arrival instructions and access code
  • About This House guide

After departure

  • Turnover inspection
  • Damage photographs and written notes
  • Extra cleaning documentation
  • Vendor estimates where needed
  • Deposit disposition within applicable agreement timing
  • Update inventory and maintenance tickets

Property Manager Mandate

Day-to-day authority

Coordinate turnovers, vendors, access codes, rental logistics, guest support, replenishment, routine inspections and approved repairs. Necessary repairs up to $1,500 may be authorized under the governance rules.

Issue escalation

Escalate safety concerns, recurring system failures, insurance-sensitive incidents, anticipated work above delegated authority and any issue likely to affect an owner stay or confirmed rental.

Property readiness

The home should be maintained in a consistently arrival-ready condition, with particular attention before every owner stay and rental turnover.

Record keeping

Maintain vendor records, invoices, inspection notes, maintenance history, warranties, access permissions and open work orders in the property record.

Housekeeping & Turnovers

Peak season

Professional weekly turnover cleaning throughout June, July and August is mandatory and shared, regardless of whether the departing occupancy was an owner or renter.

Turnover inspection

Housekeeping reports breakage, missing inventory, stains, abnormal depletion, pet/event-related cleaning, maintenance concerns and anything that could affect the next arrival.

Reset standard

Fresh linens and towels, cleaned kitchen/baths, replenished approved supplies, reset furniture and equipment, secure owner closets, thermostat reset, lock check and visible exterior review.

Off-season owner stays

Stay-specific cleaning and guest costs are charged to the owner using or facilitating the stay.
