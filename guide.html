House Essentials

A few practical details to help you settle in quickly and enjoy the house with ease.

House Notes

  • No smoking or vaping anywhere on the property.
  • Only registered guests may stay overnight.
  • We love pets that are part of the family and are happy to welcome approved pets to the house. Please make sure every pet staying on the property has been approved in advance and that the Pet Addendum has been completed and signed before arrival.
  • Please rinse beach sand outdoors before coming inside.
  • Close and secure windows and doors when leaving the house.

Parking

Parking is available for up to two vehicles in the designated parking area. Additional vehicles may be parked on the street where permitted. Please be mindful of our neighbors and take care not to block driveways, gates, or access points.

Thermostat

The thermostat is set to 70°F before your arrival and may be adjusted to your preference during your stay. We simply ask that you avoid extreme temperature settings or frequent large adjustments, as unusually high energy usage may result in an additional utility charge.

Beach gear

For your convenience, the house is stocked with beach chairs, beach towels, umbrellas, a cooler, and a selection of beach-day essentials. Please rinse off excess sand before bringing items back inside and return everything to the designated storage area after use.

Laundry

Washer and dryer facilities are available for guest use. Please use only high-efficiency (HE) detergent and avoid overfilling the detergent or softener dispenser drawers. For the best results—and to help protect the machines—please also avoid overloading either the washer or dryer.

Grill

A full propane tank and grilling tools are provided for your convenience. Please use the grill only in its designated outdoor location, keep it attended while in use, and turn off the burners and propane valve when finished. Once the grill has cooled, a quick brush of the grates is greatly appreciated so it is ready for the next use.

Family, Baby & Pet Amenities

We're delighted to welcome guests of all ages—including our littlest visitors and four-legged family members. To help make your stay more comfortable, we've provided a selection of baby, children's, and pet items. Many are the same products our own families use and enjoy, while others have been thoughtfully added to accommodate guests.

Because every child and pet has unique needs, we ask that parents and pet owners carefully inspect all equipment before use to ensure it is appropriate, properly assembled, and in good working condition. Use of these amenities is entirely at your discretion and responsibility.

Health & Safety

Tick Awareness

Ticks are common on Martha's Vineyard, particularly in grassy and wooded areas. We recommend using repellent and doing a quick tick check after time outdoors, especially for children and pets.
