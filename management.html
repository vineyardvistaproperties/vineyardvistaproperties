01

Property Operations

The core operating standards for managing the home day to day, including delegated authority, escalation, readiness, access and record keeping.

Property Manager Mandate

Day-to-day authority

Handle routine property operations, vendor coordination, guest support, approved purchasing, inspections and ordinary repairs needed to keep the home safe, functional and arrival-ready.

Issue escalation

Escalate safety concerns, material property damage, recurring system failures, anticipated work above delegated authority and any issue likely to affect a confirmed stay.

Property readiness

Maintain the home in a consistently arrival-ready condition, with particular attention to cleanliness, supplies, systems, access, exterior presentation and open maintenance items.

Record keeping

Maintain vendor records, invoices, inspection notes, maintenance history, warranties, access permissions and other operating records.

02

Rentals & Agreements

All paid occupancy is centralized through Vineyard Vista. Booking intake, agreements, pet/event approvals, deposits, guest communications, turnover and post-stay damage handling follow one process.

Every paid stay runs through the LLC framework.

No cash rentals, owner-created listings, side agreements, informal paid stays or direct payment to an individual owner.

Standard Booking Workflow

1 · Inquiry & fit

Confirm dates, party size, maximum occupancy, intended use, pet request and any party/event request.

2 · Pricing & terms

Confirm rental rate, non-refundable deposit, security deposit, $500 cleaning fee, additional fees and payment schedule.

3 · Agreement package

Issue Rental Agreement plus Pet Addendum and/or Party Addendum when applicable.

4 · Payment & clearance

Collect required funds, verify signed documents, approved occupants and operating notes before arrival.

5 · Guest access

Issue reservation-specific access and About This House guide only after the stay is cleared.

6 · Turnover & close-out

Housekeeping resets the property, documents issues and supports deposit disposition.

Key Terms

Pets

Prior approval and signed Pet Addendum required. Current standard: $250 non-refundable pet fee and $1,000 refundable pet deposit.

Events

Prior written approval and Party Addendum required. Quiet consideration is especially important from 10:00 PM–8:00 AM.

Smoking

Smoking, vaping and electronic cigarettes are prohibited inside and within 25 feet of the property.

Damage

Renter is responsible for damage caused by renter, guests or pets beyond normal wear and tear.

03

Property Management

The working operating page for the physical house: housekeeping, access, vendors, preventive maintenance, seasonal operations, repairs and capital planning.

Access & Security

Credential structure

Use separate credentials for owners, property manager, housekeeping, approved vendors and rental guests wherever the lock platform permits.

Guest access

Rental codes should be reservation-specific, time-limited or changed immediately after checkout.

Vendor access

Prefer vendor-specific or temporary codes. Permanent master codes should be tightly restricted.

Owner storage

Locked owner storage remains secured during rentals and vendor visits unless access is specifically required.

Replacement Standard

Like-for-like first.

01Exact product
Buy the same model, size, finish, color and quantity whenever available.
02Current equivalent
A packaging or model-year update may be used if substantively unchanged.
03Closest equivalent
If discontinued, use a comparable substitute only when quality, appearance and function remain consistent.
04Material change
Design, quality or brand-standard changes require approval.
