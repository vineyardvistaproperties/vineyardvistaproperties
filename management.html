Governance

The operating framework for how the three partners own, fund, use and steward the property together. This page is the management-level source for governance; detailed rental administration, house standards and physical property operations live on their own pages.

Ownership & Operating Structure

Island Holdings LLC

Parent holding company owned equally by the three partners. Each member holds one-third ownership with equal voting rights and long-term participation in the underlying real estate.

Property Address LLC

Single-purpose title-holding entity for the Martha's Vineyard residence. Future properties can be isolated in their own property LLCs.

Vineyard Vista Properties LLC

Operating and management entity for branding, rentals, bookkeeping, vendors, purchasing, guest experience and property administration.

The Residence

The physical asset held by the property LLC. It is not itself a company and should be managed as a long-term family asset first.

Financial Governance

Shared operating costs

Mortgage, taxes, insurance, utilities, peak-season cleaning, maintenance, management, common supplies and approved property-level purchases are shared according to ownership interests.

Owner-specific costs

Costs generated solely by one owner's discretionary use—especially off-season cleaning, personal provisioning, special services or damage caused by that owner or their guests—belong to that owner.

Reserve floor

Maintain the greater of $100,000 or one year of projected operating expenses before considering distributions.

First-year rental income

100% of net rental income from the first summer remains in the LLC to fund reserves, maintenance and repairs.

Distributions: only after current obligations, known near-term expenses, required reserves, necessary maintenance and approved capital projects are funded.

Owner Use & Calendar Governance

Peak-season access

Each owner receives one June week, one July week and one August week annually, with the LLC rental week rotating through each month over the four-year cycle.

Holiday rotation

Memorial Day, Fourth of July, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas/New Year's priority rotate so premium dates are shared over time.

Peak-season turnover

Professional weekly cleaning throughout June, July and August is a shared operating expense whether the week is owner-occupied or rented.

Off-season use

When available, off-season owner use requires at least 24 hours' notice. Cleaning and stay-specific costs are charged to the owner using or facilitating the stay.

Transfers, Continuity & Annual Review

Transfers & exits

Co-owners receive a right of first refusal. One owner may acquire a departing one-third interest, or the remaining owners may split it and convert to 50/50 ownership, subject to the controlling agreement.

Trusts & inheritance

Transfers to revocable trusts are permitted for estate planning. Heirs remain subject to LLC obligations, transfer rules and the right of first refusal.

Annual owner meeting

Review financials, reserve balance, rental pricing, owner calendar, property-manager performance, vendor performance, capital needs and proposed policy changes.

Document hierarchy

This portal is the working operating resource. Executed LLC agreements, loan documents, insurance policies and applicable law control if a conflict arises.
