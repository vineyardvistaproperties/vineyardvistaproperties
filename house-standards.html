Approved Products

Each item can be represented here with its image, exact product name, category, standard quantity and direct retailer link. The catalog is intentionally data-driven so products can be added or retired without redesigning the page.

Image approach. Product thumbnails should be copied into the site's own assets/products/ folder when the catalog is populated. This avoids broken retailer hotlinks and keeps the management page stable if Amazon or Williams Sonoma changes its image delivery URLs.
Replacement Standard

Like-for-like first.

01Exact product
Buy the same model, size, finish, color and quantity whenever available.
02Current equivalent
A packaging or model-year update may be used if the product is substantively unchanged.
03Closest equivalent
If discontinued, use a comparable substitute only when it does not materially alter quality, appearance or function.
04Material change
Design, quality or brand-standard changes require the applicable owner approval.
