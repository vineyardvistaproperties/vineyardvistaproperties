Family, Baby & Pet Amenities

We're delighted to welcome guests of all ages—including our littlest visitors and four-legged family members. To help make your stay more comfortable, we've provided a selection of baby, children's, and pet items. Many are the same products our own families use and enjoy, while others have been thoughtfully added to accommodate guests.

Because every child and pet has unique needs, we ask that parents and pet owners carefully inspect all equipment before use to ensure it is appropriate, properly assembled, and in good working condition. Use of these amenities is entirely at your discretion and responsibility.

Tick Awareness

Ticks are common on Martha's Vineyard, particularly in grassy and wooded areas. We recommend using repellent and doing a quick tick check after time outdoors, especially for children and pets.
