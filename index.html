Request Your Stay

A considered booking process.

Tell us who is traveling and the dates you have in mind. Vineyard Vista reviews each request before documents or payment are issued.

  1. 01
    Request

    Submit dates, party size, guest information and any pet request.

  2. 02
    Approval

    Vineyard Vista reviews availability, occupancy and stay details.

  3. 03
    Agreements

    Approved guests receive the Rental Agreement and, when applicable, a Pet Addendum.

  4. 04
    Signature & payment

    Documents are signed before payment is collected.

  5. 05
    Confirmation

    The reservation is confirmed after completed documents and payment.

  6. 06
    Pre-arrival

    Confirmed guests receive arrival details and access to About This House.

Parties, events and larger gatherings require a separate written request and Party Addendum after the house booking is confirmed.
