15 Night Heron Road · Edgartown

A renovated Katama saltbox made for easy island days.

Explore the house

The House

Classic Vineyard form, thoughtfully renewed.

A quintessential cedar-shingle saltbox renovated in 2025, 15 Night Heron offers an open-plan living space, hardwood floors, an updated kitchen with a new island, and renovated bathrooms. Mini-split systems provide heating and air conditioning throughout the house.

Outside, the property includes a patio, outdoor shower, newly shingled shed, circular shell-covered driveway, irrigation, and an existing rock pond. The Island Grove association also includes common-area maintenance and tennis courts.

The Vineyard

Katama, with Edgartown close at hand.

From 15 Night Heron, the rhythm of a Vineyard stay is easy to imagine: South Beach mornings, a quick stop in Edgartown for coffee or provisions, afternoons back at the house, and dinner in town.

The location sits within Island Grove in Edgartown, a quiet residential setting that keeps the beach, Wilson's Landing, and the village within a short drive.
