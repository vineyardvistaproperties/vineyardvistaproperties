Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

Vineyard Vista Properties ("Company," "We," "Us," or "Our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website (the "Website"), submit a stay request, or otherwise communicate with us regarding Vineyard Vista Properties and its guest services. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Website.

Information We Collect

We may collect non-personal information about you when you interact with the Website. This information may include:

  • Browser type
  • IP address
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Browsing actions and patterns

We may also collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you submit a stay request, contact us, or communicate with us regarding a potential or confirmed stay. This information may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Requested arrival and departure dates
  • Number of adults and children in the proposed party
  • Pet breed and weight when pet approval is requested
  • Reservation, billing, or payment-related information when applicable
  • Other information you choose to provide in connection with a stay or guest-services request

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect in the following ways:

  1. To Provide and Manage Services: review stay requests, communicate regarding availability, coordinate approved reservations, provide guest support, and administer services connected with a stay.
  2. To Communicate: respond to inquiries and send information relating to requested or confirmed services.
  3. To Improve the Website and Services: understand how visitors use the Website and improve its functionality, guest experience, and operations.
  4. Security and Compliance: protect the Website and our services from fraudulent activity or unauthorized access, comply with legal obligations, and enforce applicable agreements and Website terms.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or trade your personal information. We may share information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including:

  1. Service Providers: with third-party providers that assist with website hosting, communications, reservation administration, payment processing, property operations, or guest services, subject to appropriate confidentiality or data-protection obligations.
  2. Business Transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of all or a portion of our assets.
  3. Legal Compliance: when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, property, safety, guests, users, or others.

The Website may link to third-party services, including Turo for any separately offered vehicle reservation. Information you provide directly to a third party is governed by that third party's own privacy policy and practices.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect and store information about interactions with the Website. Cookies are small data files placed on your device when you visit a website. You can configure your browser to refuse some or all cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. If cookies are disabled, some portions of the Website may not function properly.

Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No security measure is perfect or impenetrable, however, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted to or stored by us.

Your Choices and Rights

You may request that we update or delete personal information you have provided to us, subject to applicable legal, contractual, recordkeeping, and operational requirements. Where marketing communications are offered, you may opt out using the unsubscribe mechanism provided or by contacting us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact Vineyard Vista Properties using the contact information provided on the Website or in your communications with us.

By using the Website or submitting information through it, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
